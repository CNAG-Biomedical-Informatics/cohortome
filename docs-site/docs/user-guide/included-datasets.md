# Pre-installed datasets

**Already on your machine.** COHORTome includes two synthetic datasets. Select either in **Datasets**, then
choose **Apply workspace**. No download is needed; database preparation is automatic.

| Dataset | Participants | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Synthetic longitudinal demo** | 38 fictional participants | Clinical queries, visits, timelines, Pheno-Ranker and imaging examples |
| **Synthetic cross-sectional demo** | 90 fictional participants: 30 RA, 30 SLE and 30 healthy controls | Molecular analyses, incomplete assay coverage and multimodal cohort selection |

:::note[For testing, not biological evidence]

Measurements and group differences are generated from predefined rules and
random variation. Results demonstrate the software; they are not biological
findings, treatment effects or clinical evidence.

:::

## Synthetic longitudinal demo

Start here to explore repeated clinical records, search for patients, save a
cohort and inspect a patient's timeline. The dataset also includes small assay
and synthetic DICOM examples for testing the linked-data views.

Patient IDs use `SYN001`–`SYN030` and `HC031`–`HC038`. Their numeric suffixes
match the ClarID subject numbers. Samples retain patient and visit identifiers.

## Synthetic cross-sectional demo

Each participant has one baseline clinical record. Different participants lack
different assays, so an analysis may include fewer patients than the saved cohort.

| Assay | Features | Samples | Values |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transcriptomics | 1,000 genes | 90 | Fractional expected-count-like values |
| DNA methylation | 128 fictional CpGs | 84 | M-values |
| Immune-cell abundance | 6 measurements | 81 | Processed cell measurements |
| Serology | 6 markers | 87 | Quantitative measurements |

Age, sex, batch and fictional recruitment centre are included in assay metadata.
Patient IDs are `DEMOAI30001`–`DEMOAI30090`, with matching ClarID subject numbers.

### Try a comparison

1. Select **Synthetic cross-sectional demo** and apply the workspace.
2. In Patients or Query, save an SLE cohort and a healthy-control cohort.
3. In Analysis, choose those cohorts and the RNA assay. Use the **RSEM
   expected-count limma-voom** workflow, not DESeq2.
4. Try methylation PCA or compare immune-cell and serology measurements.
5. In Multimodal, select the assay combination you need and save matching patients.

The RNA values were **not generated by RSEM**; their declared type lets the demo
exercise the expected-count workflow. CpG identifiers are fictional and cannot
support genomic annotation. Planted effects and correlations test the software,
not a biological hypothesis. `simulation_truth.json` records the planted effects.

## Managing the examples

Both datasets show **Included with COHORTome**. They cannot be removed or
reinstalled from the catalogue. Share a clean installation, not an existing
research runtime containing chats, credentials or imported data.

For real studies, see [Fetch and prepare](fetch-datasets.md). For prepared
archives, see [Prepared packages](downloadable-datasets.md).
