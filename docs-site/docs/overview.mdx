# COHORTome

:::tip[In one sentence]
COHORTome helps research teams connect clinical and assay
data captured once or over time, find groups of patients, and run traceable
analyses without replacing the systems that store the source data.
:::

COHORTome is a generic open-source tool that can be deployed alongside different
approved data systems. Its first consortium-scale deployment is planned in the
context of the
[3TR consortium](https://www.ihi.europa.eu/projects-results/project-factsheets/3tr).
In that deployment, COHORTome will receive authorized releases from the 3TR Omics DM
Warehouse without depending on how the warehouse stores its files. The warehouse
keeps and distributes data; COHORTome provides the research workspace.

[PRECISESADS](https://www.ihi.europa.eu/projects-results/project-factsheets/precisesads)
is held in the Omics DM Warehouse and is the first real reference implementation,
with scientific collaboration from FPS in Granada. Public synthetic fixtures
remain available for development, demonstration, and automated testing.

## What COHORTome does

- brings approved clinical and assay datasets into one research workspace
- supports both cross-sectional studies and longitudinal follow-up
- connects records from the same patient, sample, visit, or time point
- shows what data are available, what are missing, and which modalities overlap
- helps researchers find and save groups of patients for follow-up analysis
- runs reviewed statistical and bioinformatics methods and keeps their outputs
- compares patients or samples by phenotype through Pheno-Ranker
- opens linked medical images in OHIF while the original pixels remain in the
  imaging archive
- can use approved language models to help plan a query or explain a result

## How responsibilities are divided

| Layer | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Sources | The Omics DM Warehouse and other approved systems keep and distribute the original data. |
| Preparing data | COHORTome organizes clinical and assay information and connects patient, sample, and visit identifiers. |
| Controlling access | COHORTome decides which registered datasets a user may open and what information may be sent to an approved model. |
| Exploring | Researchers review timelines, clinical and assay summaries, medical images, missing data, and overlap between modalities. |
| Finding cohorts | Researchers ask questions, inspect the method used, and save reusable groups of patients or samples. |
| Analyzing | COHORTome runs reviewed clinical, omics, pathway, and phenotype-similarity methods. |
| Keeping evidence | COHORTome retains the data scope, settings, logs, plots, tables, files, and run history needed to understand a result. |

## Core workflow

1. An administrator registers an approved dataset and prepares it for searching.
2. A researcher chooses one or more datasets they are allowed to use.
3. The Dashboard shows the patient timeline, available assays, missing data,
   and overlap between modalities.
4. Query helps the researcher find a group of patients or samples and shows how
   that group was selected.
5. The researcher saves the group as a cohort and opens it in Analysis.
6. Analysis runs a reviewed method and keeps the settings, logs, plots, tables,
   and generated files with the result.
7. Where permitted, an approved language model can help plan the next question
   or explain the output without taking control of access or execution.

## Governed AI

LLM assistance is a first-class platform capability, not a general-purpose
chatbot. It supports cohort discovery, analysis planning, and interpretation
inside the current user's authorized workspace.

Administrators choose which model services are approved, including services
that run locally or inside a private network. Rules attached to each dataset
determine whether detailed information, a limited summary, or nothing at all
may be sent. COHORTome can still answer supported questions without a model and tells
the researcher which route produced the result.

Read [Governed AI](./concepts/governed-ai) for the execution and evaluation
contract.

## Current status

COHORTome currently implements the governed workspace, longitudinal dashboard,
clinical and assay exploration, Query, saved cohorts, Analysis sessions,
transcriptomics workflows, Pheno-Ranker, configurable LLM providers,
multimodal-readiness inspection, and optional OHIF/DICOMweb integration.

The multimodal feature matrix is currently a bounded inspection and
feature-engineering preview. It is not yet a validated cross-modal statistical
or machine-learning workflow. PRECISESADS ingestion, the publication
demonstrator, and formal LLM evaluation are active project work.

See [Platform Scope And Boundaries](./project/scope-and-boundaries) for a precise
current-versus-target account.

## What COHORTome is not

- the 3TR Omics DM Warehouse or another primary storage system
- an ADEx replacement
- a raw DICOM archive or MRI segmentation pipeline
- an arbitrary notebook or command-execution service
- an autonomous clinical decision-support system
- evidence that synthetic disease or treatment effects are biologically real
